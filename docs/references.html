Attribution and release terms

Cite the UGC 11487 analysis and the model's eventual versioned software release, together with the original sources of the physical inputs. A software DOI and final author list have not been assigned in this preparation package.

  • Law-Smith et al. (2020), STARS library; upstream source and data.
  • Mockler et al. (2019): TDE light-curve modelling and accretion smoothing context.
  • Draine optical data: graphite and silicate optical properties; underlying Draine & Lee (1984) and Laor & Draine (1993) calculations.
  • Mathis, Rumpl & Nordsieck (1977): size-distribution context. The present 0.1–1 μm interval is a model choice.
  • Lu et al. (2016): time-dependent TDE dust reprocessing.
  • Baskin & Laor (2018): sublimation-threshold prescription.
  • WISE / IRSA: W1 and W2 equal-energy response curves.
  • STScI CALSPEC: adopted Vega spectrum alpha_lyr_stis_011.fits.

Photometric convention

The model spectrum is redshifted and scaled by luminosity distance before integration against the official equal-energy responses. The band signal is divided by the Vega signal and multiplied by the adopted band zero point (309.540 Jy for W1; 171.787 Jy for W2). No additional photon-counting factor is applied to an already equal-energy response.
